Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Bulk wagon that only carries ores and other bulk goods.
    - Unloading pit and loading station built across the track, each with a built-in belt port and an inventory for inserters.
    - Stations turn and centre themselves on the rail when built; R rotates the belt port side while placing.
    - Startup settings for wagon slots, station sizes and port belt speed; runtime settings for transfer rate and extra bulk items.
