Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Major Features:
    - One building per wagon: the unloading bunker (under the track) and the loading silo (over it), 6 x 6 tiles each, replace the three stations per wagon.
    - Bulk connectors in every belt tier dock onto any tile of the long sides of a bunker or silo. They run at their tier's speed and never drop goods on the ground. The faster tiers unlock with the belt research.
    - Near a train stop, bunker and silo snap onto the wagon slots of a halted train; the placement indicators show the free slots.
  Changes:
    - The bunker's gratings can be walked and driven on, its rims and the silo beside the track block players and vehicles.
    - Unloading pits, belt loaders and portal silos from 0.3 are replaced on update by one bunker or silo per wagon slot, with their contents, circuit settings and colour. Where the new building has no room, the old stations stay and the spot gets a map marker; circuit wires of replaced stations have to be reconnected.
    - The old stations can no longer be crafted.

Version: 0.3.3
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - While a pit unloads a wagon, its cargo drains through the open bottom flaps: a crater over each flap with a dark opening.
  Changes:
    - Stations keep the 1-tile grid along the track, like rail signals, so three fit under every wagon of a train, not only every other one.
    - The placement hints use the game's rail signal placement indicators instead of green frames.
  Bugfixes:
    - Partly loaded wagons no longer show the hopper floor around the cargo; the cargo stays against the walls at every fill level.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.2
Date: 2026-10-25
  Changes:
    - Unloading pits in a row join edge to edge: no ground or gravel shows between them any more.
    - The pit ends in a concrete sill under the track, so plain track meets a light sill instead of the open pit.
    - The pit's gravel apron is only at its far ends now, it no longer spills onto a neighbouring pit.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Stations can be coloured: a colour panel next to the station window, or Shift + right click on a locomotive, wagon or train stop and Shift + left click on the station. Blueprints and copy-paste keep the colour.
    - Loaded wagons show how full they are (four heap heights).
    - While goods move only the belts run; the chutes stay down.
    - Portal silo: the port belt visibly runs into a hooded intake on the bucket elevator.
    - Unloading pit: gravel apron with a ragged edge blends it into the ground; its insides are only hinted at.
    - Green placement markers: while a station is in the cursor, every free spot on straight rails nearby is outlined.
  Changes:
    - Colour masks of wagon and stations now work like the vanilla ones (grey paint under the mask, colour at alpha 0.5), so they look the same as a locomotive in the same colour.
  Bugfixes:
    - The cargo heap now fits the hopper on north-south track (it sat too high).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Stations can only be built across a straight rail and snap to the rail grid like rail signals, so three fit side by side under one wagon.
    - Circuit panel: choose whether the wagon readings go out on the red and/or green network; the panel is laid out like the vanilla circuit window.
    - The station window shows the station in its preview.
    - The belt loader's window no longer shows a locked inventory slot.
    - Chutes and spouts move down when a wagon arrives and up when it leaves.
  Changes:
    - Stations collide with buildings, trees, rocks, rail signals and each other.
    - Transfer rate default is 250 items/s per station, moved in small steps every 12 ticks.
  Bugfixes:
    - Bulk wagons on north-south track no longer leave a gap to the car ahead.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Circuit network panel next to the station window.
    - Enable/disable condition: a station only loads or unloads while its condition is true.
    - Read the contents of the docked wagon, and a "wagon docked" signal (W = 1).
    - The belt loader can now be wired too (click it to open its window).
    - Blueprints keep station circuit settings and wires; Shift + right/left click copies settings between stations.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Bulk wagon: hopper wagon with three compartments that only carries ores and other bulk goods. Load is shown in the colour of the cargo. Colourable like a locomotive.
    - Belt loader (first research): no inventory, loads stopped bulk wagons straight from the connected belt at that belt's speed.
    - Portal silo loading station (second research): buffer inventory, filled by its built-in belt port or inserters.
    - Unloading pit with walkable gratings: empties stopped bulk wagons into its inventory, emptied by its built-in belt port or inserters.
    - All stations are built across the track, three per wagon, and turn and centre themselves on the rail. R switches the belt port side while placing.
    - Docking animations: spouts lower onto the wagon, flap openers rise at the pit.
    - Station inventories can be read by the circuit network.
    - Every Space Age planet gets its own variant of wagon and stations (placeholder tint for now).
    - Startup settings for wagon slots, station sizes and port belt speed; map settings for transfer rate and extra bulk items.
